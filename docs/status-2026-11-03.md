# Scene status — 2026-11-03

Dated research notes from community announcements and public project releases
through **2026-11-03**. These are community reports and author claims, not
independently verified facts. The explorer's NPDM dataset is unchanged.

- NPDM metadata remains the **2026-10-24** snapshot. No newer
  `npdm-metadata.json` appeared.
- **23.0.1 remained current at review time**; no 23.0.2 was observed.

## Tooling

- [`cpypwn/mac2jb`](https://github.com/cpypwn/mac2jb) v0.7.2 (2026-11-03)
  adds Elden Ring PC `.sl2` save conversion from a verified SaveKit backup,
  SaveKit writes for accessible save archives, large-save verification fixes,
  and in-app update fixes.
- It remains an Apple Silicon macOS helper, not an exploit or CFW. It still
  requires `ns2jb` or CivSwitch and does not provide FS, kernel access, or
  console persistence.

## Unchanged

- There is still **no public FS chain, kernel exploit, CFW, or emuNAND**.
- The official WebKit port remains private/unreleased; the public research
  payload pack's code-execution claims remain unverified.
- The Seedsow/Artemis launcher remains a semi-tethered userland path, not an
  FS entry point.

## Why this does not change the explorer data

No new system-module NPDM metadata appeared. `mac2jb` is a desktop helper and
does not supply system NPDM metadata. `data/npdm-metadata.json` and the service
graph remain at the 2026-10-24 snapshot.
