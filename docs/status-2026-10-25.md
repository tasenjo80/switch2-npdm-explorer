# Scene status — 2026-10-25

Dated research notes from the r/switch2hacks `#dev-research` and
`#hack-discussion` channels. These are community reports, not confirmed
claims. The explorer's NPDM dataset itself is unchanged.

## Data freshness

- NPDM metadata: **2026-10-24** (the source dump). No newer
  `npdm-metadata.json` appeared in the 2026-10-25 incremental export.
- Discord review: **2026-10-25 22:47 +11:00**.

## Firmware

- **23.0.1 is still current.** No newer system update was observed.
- A newer firmware is still expected eventually; the surface service is a
  likely patch target.

## WebKit

- The WebKit port of the userland exploit is still **not public**.
- `switch2exploit.xyz` remained a placeholder at the time of writing.
- Community reports say it has been tested on 23.0.1 and should also work on
  lower firmware. Treat this as unverified until a release and write-up exist.
- A possible concern: `NIM` safe-system-version checks could impose an online
  requirement over time. This is a discussion point, not a confirmed
  mechanism.

## Dumping

- `kurumuz/ns2jb` can now dump **system-title ExeFS**; system-title ROMFS and
  `.kips` are not public yet (as of this note).
- Game asset reads use the console's `fs` mount, which decrypts on demand.
  This is not the same as offline decryption.
- **NCA4** uses a different encryption scheme from NCA3. No public offline
  NCA4 decryptor or key set is available.
- `alula/beetool` is a hactool fork with Switch 2 (Ounce) and NPDM JSON
  support. It is useful for NPDM parsing, but it is not a complete NCA4
  solution.

## Reliability

Anecdotal, from a small number of testers:

- Ethernet appears significantly better than Wi-Fi.
- One tester reported **0/29 on Wi-Fi** and **5/29 on Ethernet**; another
  reported a success rate of a few in seven Ethernet runs.
- Successful runs took roughly **5–6 minutes** from RPC connect to FS demo end.
- Stage **2/4 (acquiring FS memory)** remains the usual failure point.
- A separate tester reported ~120 attempts without a successful run.

These numbers are not comparable across consoles, layouts, or forks.

## Risks

- Community reports mention at least two bricks. They appear to involve
  out-of-scope writes or experiments, not a normal run.
- One report describes a **persistent socket failure** after a failed run:
  SmileBASIC booted, but `nn::socket.connect` timed out even after reboots,
  reinstalls, and recovery-mode cache clears. The console was otherwise
  usable, but the exploit could not reconnect.
- No confirmed bans from `ns2jb` were reported in this window. Historically,
  bans can be delayed and may be based on telemetry. Staying offline while
  jailbroken is the cautious choice.
- **Do not write to NAND** and do not let an LLM drive NAND operations.

## Why this does not change the explorer data

No new system-module NPDM metadata was posted in the incremental export, so
`data/npdm-metadata.json` and the service graph remain at the 2026-10-24
snapshot. The notes above are context for future data updates, not a new
dataset.
