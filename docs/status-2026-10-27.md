Dated research notes from the r/switch2hacks `#dev-research`,
`#hack-discussion`, `#switch-2-hacking-news`, and announcements channels,
through **2026-10-27 11:23 +11:00**. These are community reports, not
confirmed claims. The explorer's NPDM dataset is unchanged.

- NPDM metadata: **2026-10-24** (the source dump). No newer

- The daily export was **complete** and contained 5,702 new-only messages,

  the `ns2jb` ROP chain.

## Firmware and browser gating

- **23.0.1 remained current**. No 23.0.2 was observed through the end of this
  export; staff said an update would be announced when it exists.
- Community analysis places the WebKit applet gate change at **21.0.0**:
  - On 21.0.0 and newer, the applet can be locked after roughly **24 hours**
    without a successful BVC check, or when a new system update is available.
  - On **20.5.0 and lower**, the older method still applies, so those consoles
    can avoid the newer lockout.
- Staff guidance for this window:
  - **20.5.0 or lower:** do not update; stay offline and/or use 90DNS.
  - **Newer firmware with SmileBASIC:** obtain the **Seedsow Lullaby** demo,
    stay offline, and/or use 90DNS.
  - **Newer firmware without SmileBASIC:** no readily available public
    entry point; waiting for another exploit is the only current option.
- 90DNS does not satisfy the BVC check. A normal nag on current firmware can
  often be cleared by briefly going online, opening eShop, restarting, and
  restoring 90DNS. A true **supernag** on older firmware with a pending update
  cannot be cleared without updating.

## Artemis Engine save path

- **Artemis Engine** games are the community's main secondary-entry-point
  bet. The underlying Lua/save vulnerability has been used in the PS4/PS5
  scene for years.
- A public **Seedsow Lullaby save editor** exists:
  <https://github.com/DialingSpoon527/seedsow-lua-editor>. It requires a
  modded, unbanned Switch 1 to create and transfer the save.
- The actual Switch 2 **Artemis entry point is not public**. The editor and
  demo are preparation, not a working jailbreak by themselves.
- At least one Artemis demo was removed from eShop during this window.
  Seedsow remained available in at least some regions.

## WebKit

- The official WebKit port remained **private/unreleased**.
- The port author was still testing on firmware below 23.0.1 and reported
  improving a read/write primitive, but no public build was released.
- Independent researchers reported several JavaScriptCore/WebKit candidates:
  a deterministic GC use-after-free in `ErrorInstance::computeErrorInfo`
  (classified as a DoS, not a primitive), a WebP out-of-bounds write, a JSC
  stack UAF, and experimental read/write primitives. None was a public
  exploit.
- The applet's JavaScriptCore is reported to be **around Safari/WebKit 15.4
  (2022)**, missing years of upstream security fixes. This is a large attack
  surface, but no chain was confirmed.

## Hardware NAND backup research

- A **UFS interposer** project is exploring a hardware modification that makes
  the soldered NAND detachable for backup and restore:
  <https://ufs-interposer.github.io/>.
- The goal is recovery from bricks and the ability to restore a known-good
  state. No confirmed, widely tested solution exists in this window.
- This is high-risk hardware work. It is not a normal-user procedure and does
  not provide a jailbreak by itself.

## Bricks and reliability

- Community reports attribute at least **two bricks** to save-data/NAND
  experiments, not to ordinary dump runs. Another report describes a
  soft-brick that recovered after a reset.
- The `ns2jb` userland exploit is still random; stage **2/4** remains the usual
  failure point. Ethernet, docked mode, and a full cold boot are common
  recommendations.
- There is still no public kernel exploit, CFW, or emuNAND. Treat NAND writes
  and save-data experiments as dangerous.

## Tooling

- **GPUBrewLauncher** is a public SmileBASIC/GPU-shader launcher for small
  custom apps: <https://github.com/NiftyNova/GPUBrewLauncher>. It does not use
  FS access and is not a general homebrew launcher.
- **mac2jb** and **NS2-Desktop** provide desktop helpers for typing/bootstrap
  and RPC setup.
- **OunceCake** remains game-only. NSO Classics dumps still fail for
  ticket/entitlement reasons. The community started a **Game Dump Hashes**
  thread to verify dumps, but the captured thread only contained methodology,
  not a verified hash list.
