independently verified facts. The explorer's NPDM dataset is unchanged.

## Public browser research bench (unverified)

- A public independent repo,
  [`Jmesmykil/switch2-captive-portal-research`](https://github.com/Jmesmykil/switch2-captive-portal-research),
  appeared on 2026-10-28 and was updated on 2026-10-31. It provides a local
  captive-portal DNS/HTTP entry and bounded browser probe pages. It is not a
  public exploit or entry point.
- The author's 2026-10-31 X posts
  ([1](https://x.com/jamesmykil/status/2116595867138638323),
  [2](https://x.com/jamesmykil/status/2116622778430595295)) describe a WebKit
  "entry" and "storage access. RPC R/w" with short videos. The contents were
  not independently verified.
- The repo's `CFW-ROADMAP.md` claims hardware-confirmed UAF, `0xB8` reclaim,
  arbitrary read, and a `CSSFontFace` leak. Its latest README says no
  console-run evidence was verified for the 31 October takeover update, that
  browser execution and support for either Switch generation remain unverified,
  and that some dashboard success labels are unreliable. The packaged bench was
  not validated on a physical Switch 2.
- On 2026-11-01 the repo added a public
  [`CVE-2026-43715` CSSFontFace trigger page](https://github.com/Jmesmykil/switch2-captive-portal-research/blob/main/cve-2026-43715-cssfontface-uaf.html).
  The CVE is a public Apple WebKit use-after-free. The page exercises the UAF
  trigger only; it does **not** include a read/write primitive or escalation,
  and the repo reports no console-run validation.
- Community Reddit posts infer FS access and 23.0.1 support from the "storage
  access" post. The repo does not support those claims; treat them as
  unverified. The official WebKit port remains private/unreleased.
- A separate community post pointed at the free game **Drift Legends 2** as a
  fast webapplet "entry." It was later reported as fake; treat it as debunked,
  not an entry point.
