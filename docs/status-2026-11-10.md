# Scene status — 2026-11-10

Dated research notes from public announcements and releases through
**2026-11-10**. These are community reports and author claims, not
independently verified facts. The explorer's NPDM dataset is unchanged.

## Data freshness

- NPDM metadata remains the **2026-10-24** snapshot. No newer
  `npdm-metadata.json` appeared.
- Nintendo released **Ver. 23.5.0** on **2026-11-09**. The official notes list
  accessibility setup, preparation for eShop gifting, Portuguese text-to-speech,
  and general fixes. The public
  [SwitchBrew 23.5.0 page](https://switchbrew.org/wiki/23.5.0) lists updated
  sysmodules and BootImagePackages, anti-downgrade fuses, and **FS** among the
  rebuilt KIPs; the security impact is not documented publicly.
- Community staff posted on 2026-11-10 that Nintendo **patched the FS bug used
  by all current public entry points** in 23.5.0 and advised staying on 23.0.1
  or lower. This is a community report, not independently verified here. Treat
  `ns2jb`, the WebKit applet, and the Artemis FS path as broken on 23.5.0 until
  tested; no public replacement or FS chain accompanied the update.
- The save-based CivSwitch and Balatro paths do not use `ns2jb`'s FS bug, but
  their 23.5.0 compatibility is also untested. Do not assume they survive the
  update; do not update if keeping the current entry points matters.

## Launcher and tooling

- **Artemis launcher v2** (2026-11-05) adds Hamidashi Creative demo support and
  fixes saves not being committed to internal storage. It still supports the
  Nora 2, Seedsow, and Hamidashi demos, exposes an HTTP eval/save API, and is a
  semi-tethered **userland** launcher, not an entry point or FS chain. The
  launcher remains demos-only; full-game support is not available.
- [`alula/Ozone`](https://github.com/alula/Ozone) and
  [`alula/libnx-ounce`](https://github.com/alula/libnx-ounce) remain early,
  source-only groundwork. Ozone ports selected Atmosphere components; its
  README says it is **not a full CFW and likely never will be**. libnx-ounce is
  a development library, not a runtime or entry point.
- [`SebaaMG/ns2jb-modloader`](https://github.com/SebaaMG/ns2jb-modloader) is a
  public RomFS/ExeFS mod loader for Switch 1 and Switch 2 games. It requires an
  existing `ns2jb`-compatible FS/RPC session and a microSD or microSD Express
  card. It supports RomFS/ExeFS replacements and IPS patches, stays active
  until reboot, and does **not** support plugins, cheats, or overlays yet.
  microSD Express still needs hardware testing. It is a modding tool, not an
  entry point or FS escalation.
- [`Luminger/balatro-switch-rce`](https://github.com/Luminger/balatro-switch-rce)
  is a public proof of concept for the **Switch 1 Balatro cartridge**. A save
  containing LÖVE entry points replaces the game with a Lua REPL and an
  unauthenticated FTP server, in the title's own userland process. The same
  save can reach the Switch 2 through Nintendo's official save transfer and runs
  in Switch 2 backward compatibility without CFW on the target. It does not
  provide a kernel exploit, sysmodule, CFW, console-wide FS, or a standalone
  entry point. It targets exact 1.0.1f/1.0.1o images; RPC/FTP are LAN-only,
  unauthenticated, and unencrypted.

## Community claims

- A **4/4 FS escalation on Seedsow Lullaby** was again described in community
  reports. No public code, bootstrap, or reproducible result was provided;
  treat it as unverified.
- Gezine's statement that the FS bug works from CivSwitch still needs a port.
  It is unverified and not a current FS path.
- The WebKit research payload pack's README describes a diagnostic payload with
  no code execution, while its commit message claims a weaponized chain. The
  claims conflict, were not independently verified, and do not establish FS or
  firmware support.

## Unchanged

- There is still **no public FS chain, kernel exploit, CFW, or emuNAND**.
- The official WebKit port remains private/unreleased.
- The Seedsow/Artemis and Balatro saves are save-persistent userland paths, not
  console-wide FS entry points.
- The public NPDM dataset does not include 23.5.0. No firmware, keys, saves, or
  raw research data are published here.

## Why this does not change the explorer data

No new system-module NPDM metadata appeared. The 23.5.0 release does not supply
system NPDM metadata, and the public tools above do not provide a new dataset.
`data/npdm-metadata.json` and the service graph remain at the 2026-10-24
snapshot.
